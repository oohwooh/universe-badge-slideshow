# Slideshow

Slideshow shows a looping slideshow of your own photos.

## Usage

Photos advance automatically every few seconds. Use LEFT and RIGHT to jump
between photos manually and SELECT to pause or resume the slideshow. Add your
own PNGs (320x240 works best) to the app's `images` directory to see them in
the show. Images are loaded one at a time as the slideshow advances, so large
photo collections won't exhaust the badge's memory. No extra hardware is
required.
